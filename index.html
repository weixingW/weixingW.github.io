I am a PhD candidate at the Hasso Plattner Institute, advised by Prof. Christoph Meinel and Prof. Gerard de Melo. I work on large multimodal language models, with a focus on hallucination detection and mitigation, unified vision-language models, and LLM post-training. I am currently an Applied Scientist Intern on Amazon’s Alexa+ post-training team. Before my PhD, I studied Electrical and Computer Engineering at the Technical University of Munich.
