Research

    Detecting and mitigating hallucinations in vision-language and diffusion language models — from editing the latents of discrete image tokens to reading the decoding trace.

    Models that understand and generate within a single architecture: mitigation methods tailored to unified VLMs, and benchmarks for whether their answers agree across tasks.

    WACV ’27

  3. C
